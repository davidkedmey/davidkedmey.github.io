How to Play

  • Click an offspring or press 1–8 to select it as the next parent
  • Each generation introduces small random mutations
  • Save favourites to the Gallery
  • Breed specimens together with ♀♂ Breed
  • Press Ctrl+Z to undo

From the Paper, and Beyond

The modes from Pixel Peppering to Full Dawkins follow the 1988 paper. The rest we added: the Extensions modes, color, mutation intensity, the ♀♂ Breed crossing, Expedition — and the orange-dashed lost ant: one offspring in every brood takes several large mutations at once, a long jump through morphospace instead of a small step.

Based on Richard Dawkins' The Evolution of Evolvability (1988)
